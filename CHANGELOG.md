# What's new

Each version of the Hnahsin web games. The newest is at the top.
Versions follow `major.minor.patch`: a new game or a big change is a new
minor version, a fix is a new patch version.

## 1.23.1 (31 October 2026)

### Aizawl Ride: cars shaped like cars

- Cars are shaped the way car designers shape them: rounded off at the
  front and back, drawn in towards the roof, with proper wheel arches and
  windows set into the body: the windscreen, the side windows between
  their pillars and the back window.
- A new car on the road: a Dzire-style saloon with a short boot, along
  with the hatchbacks, the taxis and the Boleros.
- The wheels of every car, bus and two-wheeler turn as they go.
- Riders look more like people: fuller jackets and arms, jeans and other
  trousers, and now and then a school bag on the back.

## 1.23.0 (31 October 2026)

### Aizawl Ride: a new delivery scooter, Aizawl's two-wheelers and a new bus

- Your scooter is new: smooth curved panels, a chrome V on the front, a
  grab rail, and a big yellow delivery box with Hnahsin on its back and
  sides. Its lights show clearly now.
- Four kinds of two-wheeler share the road: scooters, commuter
  motorbikes, Royal Enfields (round chrome lamp, deep mudguards, spoked
  wheels) and sports bikes. Like on Aizawl's roads, they are now most of
  the traffic, and now and then someone rides behind.
- Every rider wears a helmet with a visor.
- The bus is now a city midi bus: a raked windscreen with the route
  board over it, a band of dark windows, a white roof with the air
  conditioner, stripes along the side and the door at the front.

## 1.22.2 (31 October 2026)

### Aizawl Ride: clean walls and a sharper picture

- At the foot of a high wall beside the road there are no more green
  peaks standing up in front of it.
- Every house stands the same short way back from the footpath; before,
  some stood well back and others came close.
- A phone that was slow once (hot, or busy with something else) no longer
  keeps the softer picture for good: on another day it tries the sharper
  one again.
- Checked on every road in the game.

## 1.22.1 (31 October 2026)

### Aizawl Ride: houses apart, a clear footpath

- No house stands half inside another any more: on the inside of a bend,
  or where two stretches of road come close, a house that would is left
  out. Houses side by side in a row still touch, as in town.
- The place-name signs and the traffic signs stand out past the footpath,
  not in the middle of it, so people walk by them.
- Ferns and small bushes keep off the footpaths.
- People walking along turn back where the footpath ends, instead of
  walking on beside the road.
- Checked on every road in the game.

## 1.22.0 (31 October 2026)

### Aizawl Ride: trees that look like trees

- The trees are made the way trees grow: a trunk that bends a little and
  forks into branches, and branches that get thinner towards the end, with
  bunches of leaves on them. Inside and under the leaves it is darker, as
  in a real tree. Bamboo, banana and pine trees are shaded the same way.
- Two new kinds of tree: a wide, spreading one and a tall, narrow one.
- The leaves are new pictures, lighter at the tip and with a vein down the
  middle.
- Ferns, grass and small bushes grow on the ground beside the road.
- On a slope, a house now goes all the way down to the ground at its
  lowest side, the way houses are built in Aizawl. No house is left
  floating over the hillside.
- Checked on every road in the game.

## 1.21.6 (31 October 2026)

### Aizawl Ride: no holes beside the road

- There is ground under it all now, within 40 m of the road. Inside a tight
  bend, or between two stretches of road close together, you no longer
  look down through a gap to the valley floor far below.
- Checked on every road in the game.

## 1.21.5 (31 October 2026)

### Aizawl Ride: people stay on the footpath

- People walk only where a footpath is drawn: not where the road meets
  another stretch of itself, where there is none. Someone walking along
  turns back where the footpath ends.
- Zebra crossings are only where there is a footpath on both sides.

## 1.21.4 (31 October 2026)

### Aizawl Ride: roads that meet properly

- Where the road comes back past itself (hairpins, and the places where a
  delivery turns back along the same street), the hillside, kerbs,
  footpaths and walls of one stretch no longer lie on the other. The road
  is drawn once where two passes overlap, and they meet without a step;
  the scooter and the traffic ride right on it.
- On the inside of the tightest bends the road no longer folds over itself.
- Houses keep off the footpaths of other stretches, and a sign is never left
  standing on a road or inside a house.
- The straight bits of road before the start and after the end stop where
  they would meet the road.
- Every road in the game has been checked.

## 1.21.3 (30 October 2026)

### Aizawl Ride: trees, poles and wires in their places

- No more trees growing inside houses: the houses along a road are now
  worked out first, and the trees keep out of them.
- The electric poles stand at the kerb, as they do in Aizawl, out of the
  way of people walking on the footpath, and never on a road, even inside a
  tight bend.
- Wires no longer pass through houses or hang low over a road; a span that
  would is left out.
- Checked on every road in the game.

## 1.21.2 (30 October 2026)

### Aizawl Ride: fixes

- No more houses, trees or poles standing in the road. Aizawl's roads loop
  round the hills and come back past themselves, and something placed
  beside one stretch could land on another (on the way to Chhinga Veng, for
  one). Every road in the game is now checked, and nothing stands on any of
  them.
- Changing lanes is a little slower, about half a second, and still smooth.

## 1.21.1 (30 October 2026)

### Aizawl Ride: a smoother lane change

- Changing lanes moves the scooter along a smooth curve, as lane games do:
  it eases in, eases out and arrives in about a third of a second, with no
  creeping at the end. A second tap in the middle of a move carries on
  smoothly to the next lane.
- The scooter leans into the move but turns only a little, and the camera
  follows less to the side, so the road stays steady.

## 1.21.0 (30 October 2026)

### Aizawl Ride: vehicles and people made like the real ones

- The cars are modelled on Aizawl's own: Maruti-style hatchbacks (the taxis
  yellow, with a roof sign), Bolero-style jeeps with the spare wheel on the
  back door, the city bus with its door on the left, and Splendor-style
  motorbikes. They have pillars round the windows, door seams and handles,
  mirrors, bumpers, grilles and alloy wheels with spokes.
- The scooter is an Activa, with a front mudguard, a chrome exhaust and
  indicators.
- On phones that can manage it, the paint shines under a clear coat, and
  the glass and chrome mirror the sky and the hills, changing with the
  light of the day.
- People walk properly now: legs swing from the hip and bend at the knee,
  arms swing and bend at the elbow. They have shaped bodies, necks, hands
  and shoes, and short hair, long hair or a bun.
- Women wear a striped puan and a blouse. New people on the footpaths: a
  schoolgirl in her puan with a school bag, a man in a jacket, and an elder
  who stoops a little and walks with a stick.
- The motorbike riders and the scooter rider sit properly, hands on the
  grips.

## 1.19.0 (30 October 2026)

### Aizawl Ride: smoother and more lifelike

- Changing lanes is smooth now: the scooter eases into the new lane and

### Aizawl Ride: veng open as your points grow

- Your points are your best scores on every road, added up. Dawrpui and the

  gained and any veng that opened.
- Luangmual, Dinthar and Tuikual North can now be reached: a new delivery
  goes from Zarkawt to Luangmual. Before, no delivery went there.

## 1.11.0 (26 October 2026)

### La siam, the famous puans, and smoother Touch and learn

- New in Touch and learn: **La siam**, making thread. Nu takes the cotton
  through six steps on a bamboo verandah: herawt (cotton gin), lasai (cotton
  bow), hmui (spinning wheel), ladawhkilh (skein pegs) and buhhawp tui
  (rice starch water), suvel (yarn swift) and lazarna (warping posts). Tap a
  tool to see its name and what it does, or let her do it all, then take the
  thread to the loom.
- Puan tah: every part of the loom now has its proper Mizo name, with a
  line on what it does.
- Puan tah: the five famous Mizo puans hang on the wall: puanchei, pawndum,
  ngotekherh, hmaram and tawlhlohpuan. Tap one to read what it means, and
  watch pi weave it row by row, with its own stripes and patterns.
- Touch and learn looks and moves more smoothly: the camera follows the
  work, round things have soft light, shadows are softer, people breathe,
  blink and look at their hands, and it draws 30 pictures a second (fewer on
  a slow phone, so it never stutters).

## 1.7.0 (25 October 2026)

### New in the Playground: Word quests

- **Word dungeon.** Rova fights monsters with Mizo names: sazu (rat), bak
  (bat), rul (snake) and more. Make a word from 16 letter tiles to hit the
  monster. Long words and rare letters hit harder, and a gold tile is a
  critical hit. Each floor ends with a boss with a trick: awle (crocodile)
  stops short words, saphai (python) heals, sakei (tiger) curses tiles.
  Between fights, buy charms in the shop.
- **Falling letters.** Drag across touching letters to make words. The tiles
  vanish and the ones above fall. A new row comes up every third word; if a
  column reaches the top, the game is over. Stones and bombs make it harder.
- AW, CH and NG are one tile each, as in the Mizo alphabet. Words are checked
  against 5,627 Mizo words, and the meaning shows when we know it.
- The Playground has 31 games now, and the site 41.

## 1.5.0 (24 October 2026)

### New in Touch and learn: Puan tah

- A Mizo backstrap loom in a room. Pi sits on a mat with the loom's belt
  (kâwnghrên) round her back and weaves a puan row by row: the warp opens,
  the shuttle (khawhthei) carries the yarn across, and the beating stick

- Pick the yarn from six Mizo colour words: sen, dum, vâr, hring, dumpâwl

## 1.0.0 (23 October 2026)

The first release.

### Games

- **Rova's Journey.** Rova runs across Mizoram in five levels: Tlang, Aizawl,
  Bazar, Champhai and Phawngpui. Jump into the door with the right Mizo
  word. Each level ends with a sentence or a Mizo saying.
- **Hnahsin Playground**, 21 games:
  - word puzzles: Spelling bee, Waffle, Strands, Word tubes, Sort it
  - quick games: Rova run, Bubble pop, Whack the rat
  - everyday Mizo: Bazar, I Spy, Count, Dress Rova, Big and small, Days and
    times, Rova says, Colour it, Rova's family, Weather report, Food stall
  - sentences and sayings: Sentence builder, Saying match
- **The Hnahsin app** as a preview in the browser.

### Words

- 1,254 words, 383 sentences and 203 sayings from the Hnahsin app.
- 1,928 more words from J.F. Laldailova's dictionary, with emoji and photos
  for the ones that can be pictured.
- Real photos for 96 words.

### Look and feel

- A home page with Rova and Mami playing tag on a flowery hill.
- Rova drawn by hand: he runs, jumps, and gets dressed in Dress Rova.
- Drawn scenes: a Mizo hill village, a dirt road, rat holes, a meadow and a
  market stall.
