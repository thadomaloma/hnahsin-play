# What's new

Each version of the Hnahsin web games. The newest is at the top.
Versions follow `major.minor.patch`: a new game or a big change is a new
minor version, a fix is a new patch version.

## 1.17.0 (26 October 2026)

### Aizawl Ride: people, zebra crossings and more lifelike vehicles

- Footpaths in town, with people walking: students with school bags, women
  in a puan, someone with an umbrella and more.
- Zebra crossings, with a sign before them. Stop and let people cross to
  earn zah (respect) and 5 coins; ride through and they hop back, and you
  hear fimkhur (careful).
- A new badge: Let people cross.
- Cars, taxis, SUVs, buses and bikes are now drawn in detail, with shine on
  the body, glass, mirrors, number plates, headlights and glowing brake
  lights, and still run smoothly.

## 1.16.0 (26 October 2026)

### Aizawl Ride: a new HUD on the road

- The HUD now sits on the road in light glass pills, so the road is bigger
  on a phone.
- A strip along the top shows what is ahead: the places on the way, traffic
  jams (🚦) and the end (🏁), with you moving along it. A warning tells you
  how far the next jam is.
- A speedometer dial with a needle: green, amber when fast, red when you
  brake.
- A mini-map shows the real shape of the road, north up, with how far you
  have come.

## 1.15.0 (26 October 2026)

### Aizawl Ride: traffic, jams and Aizawl traffic discipline

- Cars, taxis, SUVs, buses and bikes now share the road: on our side (we keep
  left) and coming the other way. They slow down behind each other and go
  round obstacles.
- A Brake button (hold it), or the down arrow, S or the space bar.
- Traffic jams at busy places, with a "No honking" sign before them. The
  queue waits, then moves off from the front.
- Aizawl traffic discipline: wait your turn in the queue and earn
  dawhtheihna (patience) and 10 coins. Going round the queue on the other
  side earns nothing. There is no horn.
- A new mission (wait your turn in 3 jams) and a badge (Aizawl driver).
- The pause button moved to the corner of the road.

## 1.14.1 (26 October 2026)

- Shorter map credits on the Aizawl Ride page, with a link to the full

## 1.14.0 (26 October 2026)

### Aizawl Ride: the Aizawl map, and a smoother ride

- New: **the Aizawl map**. Deliver parcels between 18 veng, from Dawrpui and
  Chanmari to Ramhlun, Luangmual and Khatla, along real roads (one-way roads
  stay one way). Each delivery takes you to that veng, so you can ride your
  way round the city. Each veng is placed at its main church or another
  landmark.
- Houses on the Aizawl map stand where they really are and as tall as they
  really are, from Google Open Buildings: Aizawl's tall buildings show, with
  windows on every floor.
- A new mission (3 deliveries on the map) and two badges (5 veng, every veng).
- Smoother riding: the road no longer jumps on bends, far houses fade in out
  of the haze, and near houses slide away to the side instead of vanishing.

## 1.13.1 (26 October 2026)

- Fixed Aizawl Ride not moving on a browser that blocks saved data: the
  play counter could stop the ride from starting. The counter can no
  longer stop any game.

## 1.13.0 (26 October 2026)

### Aizawl Ride: wallet, garage, missions and badges

- The coins you pick up now go into a wallet when the ride ends, once per
  ride. Your wallet shows at the top.
- A garage with five scooters named by Mizo colours: hring (green), sen
  (red), dumpâwl (blue), vâr (white) and dum (black). Buy them with your
  coins. They look different and all ride the same.
- Six missions with coin rewards, such as riding for a whole minute or
  delivering to Chawnga Road. Each reward can be claimed once.
- Six badges, including one for meeting all five Mizo words of the road.
- A pause button, and a "Reset my progress" button that asks first.
- Your progress is kept safely on this device; older best scores carry over.

## 1.12.0 (26 October 2026)

### Aizawl Ride

- New game: **Aizawl Ride**. Deliver a parcel by scooter along real Aizawl
  roads, from Millennium Centre to Civil Hospital (1.4 km) or to Chawnga Road
  (3.1 km). Steer between the lanes, dodge rocks, logs and roadblocks, and
  pick up coins.
- The road's bends and hills, the hillsides beside it and the houses along it
  come from real map data (OpenStreetMap and Copernicus elevation), prepared
  in the Zoram Delivery project. Roadside signs show real places such as
  Solomon's Cave, Vanapa Hall and the Head Post Office.
- The parcel has a Mizo name (alu, artui, purun…), and Mizo words come up as
  you ride: tlang, ruam, romei, mau and tlai.
- The light turns golden towards the end of the ride, and part of the road
  runs through morning haze.
- Works on a phone screen without scrolling, in light and dark mode, and
  stays smooth on slower phones.

## 1.11.0 (26 October 2026)

### La siam, the famous puans, and smoother Touch and learn

- New in Touch and learn: **La siam**, making thread. Nu takes the cotton
  through six steps on a bamboo verandah: herawt (cotton gin), lasai (cotton
  bow), hmui (spinning wheel), ladawhkilh (skein pegs) and buhhawp tui
  (rice starch water), suvel (yarn swift) and lazarna (warping posts). Tap a
  tool to see its name and what it does, or let her do it all, then take the
  thread to the loom.
- Puan tah: every part of the loom now has its proper Mizo name, with a
  line on what it does.
- Puan tah: the five famous Mizo puans hang on the wall: puanchei, pawndum,
  ngotekherh, hmaram and tawlhlohpuan. Tap one to read what it means, and
  watch pi weave it row by row, with its own stripes and patterns.
- Touch and learn looks and moves more smoothly: the camera follows the
  work, round things have soft light, shadows are softer, people breathe,
  blink and look at their hands, and it draws 30 pictures a second (fewer on
  a slow phone, so it never stutters).

## 1.10.0 (26 October 2026)

## 1.8.0 (25 October 2026)

### New in the Playground: Crossword cave

- A crossword in the dark. Only the first word is lit; each word you solve
  lights up the words that cross it, on the way to the exit (🚪).
- Clues are the English meaning. Spell the Mizo word with letter tiles, no
  typing needed.
- Chests (💰) give a hint. Monsters (👹) must be answered within 30 seconds.

- Every floor is a new crossword, made from 817 of the app's own words.

## 1.7.0 (25 October 2026)

### New in the Playground: Word quests

- **Word dungeon.** Rova fights monsters with Mizo names: sazu (rat), bak
  (bat), rul (snake) and more. Make a word from 16 letter tiles to hit the
  monster. Long words and rare letters hit harder, and a gold tile is a
  critical hit. Each floor ends with a boss with a trick: awle (crocodile)
  stops short words, saphai (python) heals, sakei (tiger) curses tiles.
  Between fights, buy charms in the shop.
- **Falling letters.** Drag across touching letters to make words. The tiles
  vanish and the ones above fall. A new row comes up every third word; if a
  column reaches the top, the game is over. Stones and bombs make it harder.
- AW, CH and NG are one tile each, as in the Mizo alphabet. Words are checked
  against 5,627 Mizo words, and the meaning shows when we know it.
- The Playground has 31 games now, and the site 41.

## 1.5.0 (24 October 2026)

### New in Touch and learn: Puan tah

- A Mizo backstrap loom in a room. Pi sits on a mat with the loom's belt
  (kâwnghrên) round her back and weaves a puan row by row: the warp opens,
  the shuttle (khawhthei) carries the yarn across, and the beating stick
  (vuakfung) packs it in.
- Pick the yarn from six Mizo colour words: sen, dum, vâr, hring, dumpâwl
  and sendang. Tap a ball of yarn in the basket or a colour button.
- Add a pattern (ziarâng), keep weaving, and finish (zo) the puan to fold
  it onto the wooden chest (thingrem).
- Tabs at the top switch between the house and the loom.
- 79 words in Touch and learn now. Khawhthei comes from J.F. Laldailova's
  dictionary; the rest are from the Hnahsin app.

## 1.0.0 (23 October 2026)

The first release.

### Games

- **Rova's Journey.** Rova runs across Mizoram in five levels: Tlang, Aizawl,
  Bazar, Champhai and Phawngpui. Jump into the door with the right Mizo
  word. Each level ends with a sentence or a Mizo saying.
- **Hnahsin Playground**, 21 games:
  - word puzzles: Spelling bee, Waffle, Strands, Word tubes, Sort it
  - quick games: Rova run, Bubble pop, Whack the rat
  - everyday Mizo: Bazar, I Spy, Count, Dress Rova, Big and small, Days and
    times, Rova says, Colour it, Rova's family, Weather report, Food stall
  - sentences and sayings: Sentence builder, Saying match
- **The Hnahsin app** as a preview in the browser.

### Words

- 1,254 words, 383 sentences and 203 sayings from the Hnahsin app.
- 1,928 more words from J.F. Laldailova's dictionary, with emoji and photos
  for the ones that can be pictured.
- Real photos for 96 words.

### Look and feel

- A home page with Rova and Mami playing tag on a flowery hill.
- Rova drawn by hand: he runs, jumps, and gets dressed in Dress Rova.
- Drawn scenes: a Mizo hill village, a dirt road, rat holes, a meadow and a
  market stall.
- Made for phones first, in light and dark mode, with clear text.

### Works anywhere

- Add it to your phone's home screen and play without internet.
- Updates show the next time you open it.
- Counts which games are played, without cookies or anything personal.
