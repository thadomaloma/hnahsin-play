# What's new

Each version of the Hnahsin web games. The newest is at the top.
Versions follow `major.minor.patch`: a new game or a big change is a new
minor version, a fix is a new patch version.

## 1.0.0 (23 October 2026)

The first release.

### Games

- **Rova's Journey.** Rova runs across Mizoram in five levels: Tlang, Aizawl,
  Bazar, Champhai and Phawngpui. Jump into the door with the right Mizo
  word. Each level ends with a sentence or a Mizo saying.
- **Hnahsin Playground**, 21 games:
  - word puzzles: Spelling bee, Waffle, Strands, Word tubes, Sort it
  - quick games: Rova run, Bubble pop, Whack the rat
  - everyday Mizo: Bazar, I Spy, Count, Dress Rova, Big and small, Days and
    times, Rova says, Colour it, Rova's family, Weather report, Food stall
  - sentences and sayings: Sentence builder, Saying match
- **The Hnahsin app** as a preview in the browser.

### Words

- 1,254 words, 383 sentences and 203 sayings from the Hnahsin app.
- 1,928 more words from J.F. Laldailova's dictionary, with emoji and photos
  for the ones that can be pictured.
- Real photos for 96 words.

### Look and feel

- A home page with Rova and Mami playing tag on a flowery hill.
- Rova drawn by hand: he runs, jumps, and gets dressed in Dress Rova.
- Drawn scenes: a Mizo hill village, a dirt road, rat holes, a meadow and a
  market stall.
- Made for phones first, in light and dark mode, with clear text.

### Works anywhere

- Add it to your phone's home screen and play without internet.
- Updates show the next time you open it.
- Counts which games are played, without cookies or anything personal.
