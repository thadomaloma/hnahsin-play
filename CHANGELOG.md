# What's new

Each version of the Hnahsin web games. The newest is at the top.
Versions follow `major.minor.patch`: a new game or a big change is a new
minor version, a fix is a new patch version.

## 1.3.0 (23 October 2026)

### Touch and learn: time, weather and people

- **Times of day.** One button moves the day on: morning (zîng), daytime
  (chhun), evening (tlai) and night (zân). The sky changes, the sun rises on
  the left and sets on the right, and the shadows turn with it.
- **Wind (thli).** The bamboo bends, the tree and flowers sway, leaves blow
  past and the washing flies.
- **Rainbow (chhimbâl)** after the rain.
- **The family.** Nu (mother) sweeps the porch with a broom, pa (father)
  walks to the gate, pi (grandmother) washes clothes in a tub of water, and
  naupang (child) chases the pig. Tap them to see what they are doing. At
  night they are inside, by the hearth.
- **Rova and Mami visit.** They come in at the gate and play tag in the yard.
- **Labels** now show one group at a time: House, Yard, Family, Sky.
- 59 words now, all from the Hnahsin app.

## 1.2.0 (23 October 2026)

### Touch and learn: more to find

- **Look inside.** The roof lifts off and the near walls come down, so you
  can see the room: the kitchen hearth with its pot, a bed with a Mizo puan,
  a table with a lamp, a chair and a mat. At night the lamp lights the room,
  and the fire on the hearth sends up smoke.
- **The yard.** A hens' coop, a pig that trots by the house, and eggs the hen
  lays. Tap an egg to put it in the basket.
- Looking inside, the labels and Find it name only the things in the room.
- 41 words now, all from the Hnahsin app.

### Fixes

- Parts drawn as plain boxes inside a group (like the coop's walls) now show
  their name when tapped.

## 1.1.0 (23 October 2026)

### New: Touch and learn

- A Mizo house on stilts, with its yard, drawn in 3D with a hand-inked look.
- Tap any part to see its Mizo name: door, window, roof, floor, wall, lamp,
  cat, hen, basket, path, fence, tree, bamboo, flowers, hills and the sky.
- Buttons with Mizo words: open the door and windows, make it night or
  morning, make it rain, light the fire.
- Labels show every part's Mizo name. Find it asks you to tap a named part
  and gives a star for each one you find.
- Drag to turn the house, or let it turn by itself. Pick the roof colour.
- All 30 words come from the Hnahsin app.

## 1.0.0 (23 October 2026)

The first release.

### Games

- **Rova's Journey.** Rova runs across Mizoram in five levels: Tlang, Aizawl,
  Bazar, Champhai and Phawngpui. Jump into the door with the right Mizo
  word. Each level ends with a sentence or a Mizo saying.
- **Hnahsin Playground**, 21 games:
  - word puzzles: Spelling bee, Waffle, Strands, Word tubes, Sort it
  - quick games: Rova run, Bubble pop, Whack the rat
  - everyday Mizo: Bazar, I Spy, Count, Dress Rova, Big and small, Days and
    times, Rova says, Colour it, Rova's family, Weather report, Food stall
  - sentences and sayings: Sentence builder, Saying match
- **The Hnahsin app** as a preview in the browser.

### Words

- 1,254 words, 383 sentences and 203 sayings from the Hnahsin app.
- 1,928 more words from J.F. Laldailova's dictionary, with emoji and photos
  for the ones that can be pictured.
- Real photos for 96 words.

### Look and feel

- A home page with Rova and Mami playing tag on a flowery hill.
- Rova drawn by hand: he runs, jumps, and gets dressed in Dress Rova.
- Drawn scenes: a Mizo hill village, a dirt road, rat holes, a meadow and a
  market stall.
- Made for phones first, in light and dark mode, with clear text.

### Works anywhere

- Add it to your phone's home screen and play without internet.
- Updates show the next time you open it.
- Counts which games are played, without cookies or anything personal.
