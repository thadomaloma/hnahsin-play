Floor 1

The cave is dark. Tap a lit word, read its clue, and tap the letter tiles to spell the Mizo word. Each word you solve lights up the words that cross it. Reach the exit 🚪 to go down a floor. 💰 holds a hint; 👹 must be answered before its clock runs out. A wrong word costs a heart. Tap a letter you placed to take it back; tap a crossing square again to switch between across and down.
